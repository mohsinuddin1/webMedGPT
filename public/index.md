# MedGPT — AI Health Assistant

> Understand Medical Reports, Blood Tests, and Medications Instantly.

MedGPT is an AI-powered health educational assistant designed to translate complex medical reports, laboratory panels, prescriptions, and health queries into clear, easy-to-understand language.

Download MedGPT on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.droploop.medgpt

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Analyze Medical Reports**: Upload and parse blood tests (CBC, lipid profile, metabolic panels, thyroid tests) for plain-English explanations.
- **Medicine Information**: Monograph insights covering dosages, mechanisms, indications, contraindications, and drug interactions.
- **Symptom Explorer**: Explore possible conditions and prepare structured questions for your doctor.
- **Doctor Visit Preparation**: Generate tailored questions and symptom chronologies to maximize clinical appointments.
- **Medical Jargon Translation**: Convert complex Latin prefixes and clinical terminology into accessible terms.
- **Private & Secure**: Health data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is MedGPT?
MedGPT is an AI-powered health assistant application that helps individuals understand medical reports, lab results, medications, and health queries in plain language.

### Can MedGPT replace my doctor?
No. MedGPT is an educational companion. It is not a diagnostic tool or substitute for professional medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or medical treatment. Always consult with a licensed healthcare clinician.

### How does MedGPT analyze lab tests?
MedGPT evaluates biomarkers against standard age- and sex-adjusted reference intervals, highlights out-of-range metrics, and outlines potential physiological factors to discuss with a physician.
