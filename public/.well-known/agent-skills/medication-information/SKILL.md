# Medication Information Skill

This skill allows AI agents to query drug monographs, understand therapeutic indications, identify contraindications, and provide patients with safe medication guidance.

## Capabilities

1. **Drug Monograph Lookup**: Retrieve generic and brand names, standard therapeutic dose ranges, and common administration schedules.
2. **Side Effect Profiling**: Categorize common versus severe adverse reactions and highlight warning signs requiring prompt medical evaluation.
3. **Interaction Screening**: Screen combinations of medications, supplements, and dietary substances for potential interactions.

## Safety & Disclaimer

MedGPT medication guidance is provided for educational purposes only. Never alter medication schedules or dosages without direct physician or pharmacist consultation.
