# Medical Jargon Simplifier Skill

This skill assists AI agents in converting dense physician discharge notes, radiological impressions, pathology reports, and clinical terms into accessible explanations.

## Capabilities

1. **Jargon Translation**: Translate Latin prefixes, clinical suffixes, and medical terminology into plain 6th-to-8th grade reading level English.
2. **Contextual Analogies**: Provide clear physiological analogies that help non-specialist patients understand how organs and biological systems function.
3. **Appointment Preparation**: Formulate tailored follow-up queries that empower patients to communicate effectively with their medical team.

## Safety & Disclaimer

Informational only. Medical simplifications should not replace consultation with certified healthcare clinicians.
