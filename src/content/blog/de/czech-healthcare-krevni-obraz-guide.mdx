Ob als Grenzgänger, Expat in Prag oder Brünn oder bei der Durchsicht medizinischer Unterlagen für Angehörige: Tschechische Laborbefunde (*výsledkový list*) weisen spezifische Eigenheiten auf.

In the Czech healthcare system, complete blood counts are ordered as **Krevní obraz (KO)**, with public funding managed primarily by **VZP (111)** via official doctor requisition slips called **žádanky**. Furthermore, Czech laboratories use the SI unit **microkatal per liter (µkat/l)** for routine hepatic and cardiac enzymes.

---

## 1. Das tschechische Laborsystem: Praktický Lékař, Žádanka und VZP

- **Praktický lékař pro dospělé (GP):** Acts as the primary care gatekeeper. Covered tests require an official paper or electronic requisition slip (**žádanka o laboratorní vyšetření**).
- **Public Health Insurance (Zdravotní pojišťovny):** Fully covered for clinical indications by **VZP (111)**, **VoZP (201)**, **ČPZP (205)**, **OZP (207)**, and **ZP MV ČR (211)** according to [VZP preventive guidelines](https://www.vzp.cz/pojistenci/prevence).
- **Commercial Walk-In Testing (Samoplátci):** Available without referral at private diagnostic networks including **Synlab**, **EUC Laboratoře**, **Aeskulab**, and **Spadia**.

---

## 2. Krevní Obraz (KO): Das tschechische Blutbild im Detail

The foundation of Czech hematology testing is **Krevní obraz (KO)**. For foundational principles, explore our [comprehensive guide to reading blood test results](/blog/how-to-read-blood-test-results). Czech laboratories follow reference standards from the [Czech Society of Clinical Biochemistry (ČSKB)](https://www.cskb.cz/doporuceni/) and [NIH MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/complete-blood-count-cbc/):

| Parameter | Abbreviation | Evaluates | Czech Reference Range | Units |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leukocyty** | WBC / Leu | White blood cells | **4.0 – 10.0** | 10⁹/l |
| **Erytrocyty** | RBC / Ery | Red blood cells | **4.0 – 5.8** (Men)<br />**3.8 – 5.2** (Women) | 10¹²/l |
| **Hemoglobin** | Hb / HGB | Oxygen-binding protein | **135 – 175** (Men)<br />**120 – 160** (Women) | **g/l** |
| **Hematokrit** | Hct / HCT | Ratio of blood cells | **0.40 – 0.50** (Men)<br />**0.35 – 0.47** (Women) | Decimal |
| **MCV** | MCV | Mean red cell volume | **82 – 98** | fl |
| **MCH** | MCH | Hemoglobin per cell | **28 – 34** | pg |
| **Trombocyty** | PLT / Tro | Platelets (clotting) | **150 – 400** | 10⁹/l |

For detailed differential insights, consult our guide on [understanding CBC differential WBC types](/blog/understanding-cbc-differential-wbc-types).

### Sedimentace Erytrocytů (FW)
- Measured via the Fahraeus-Westergren method in **mm/hod**. Normal: Men < 15 mm/hod; Women < 20 mm/hod.

## 3. Základní Biochemie: Das tschechische Enzym-Spezifikum in µkat/l

Czech laboratories report enzyme activity in **µkat/l** rather than standard international **U/l**:

```
==================================================================
CONVERSION:  1 µkat/l  ≈  60 U/l   |   1 U/l  ≈  0.0167 µkat/l
Example: ALT 0.60 µkat/l × 60 = 36 U/l (Normal)
==================================================================
```

| Biomarker | Purpose | Czech Normal Range | Conversion Equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glukóza (Glykémie)** | Blood sugar | **3.9 – 5.6 mmol/l** | 70 – 100 mg/dL |
| **Kreatinin** | Kidney filtration | **62 – 106 µmol/l** (Men)<br />**44 – 80 µmol/l** (Women) | 0.7 – 1.2 mg/dL |
| **Močovina (Urea)** | Nitrogen breakdown | **2.5 – 8.3 mmol/l** | 7 – 20 mg/dL BUN |
| **ALT** | Liver enzyme | **< 0.80 µkat/l** (Men)<br />**< 0.60 µkat/l** (Women) | < 48 U/l |
| **AST** | Liver & muscle | **< 0.70 µkat/l** (Men)<br />**< 0.55 µkat/l** (Women) | < 42 U/l |
| **CRP** | Acute inflammation | **< 5.0 mg/l** | < 0.5 mg/dL |

For renal markers, see our guide on [how to read kidney function test results](/blog/how-to-read-kidney-function-test-results).

## 4. Labormarkierungen und Symbole (+, -, H, L)

- **`+` / `-` or `H` / `L`:** Above or below the statistical 95% reference interval.
- Minor isolated deviations are common; review our guide on [abnormal blood test results and when to worry](/blog/abnormal-blood-test-results-when-to-worry).

## 5. Notfall-Warnsignale: Wann sofort Hilfe nötig ist

> **Emergency Warning:** Call **155** or **112** if results coincide with acute symptoms:
> - Hemoglobin **< 70 g/l** with dizziness or severe shortness of breath.
> - Platelets **< 20 × 10⁹/l** with spontaneous bleeding or petechiae.
> - Potassium **> 6.5 mmol/l** (cardiac arrhythmia risk).
> - Creatinine **> 350 µmol/l** with anuria.

## 8. Häufig gestellte Fragen (FAQ)

### What is the difference between krevní obraz and biochemie?
Krevní obraz (KO) measures cellular blood components (RBC, WBC, platelets), while biochemie measures chemical substances in serum (glucose, creatinine, liver enzymes).

### How do I convert Czech liver enzymes in µkat/l to international U/l?
Multiply the value in µkat/l by approximately 60 (e.g., 0.50 µkat/l × 60 ≈ 30 U/l).
